Switch emulation on the Mac is good now. You can play Fire Emblem: Three Houses at 4K at your desk. But the Switch is still the thing you take on the train, and the two don't talk to each other: saves live in different places and nothing carries over.

**WarpPoint is the link.** Install it on both sides once. After that, close the game on the Mac, pick up the Switch, and your save is already there. Then go back the other way.

<Note label="the rule">Zero UI when it's working, clear UI when it needs you. No IPs to type, no SD card swaps.</Note>

## How it works

There are two halves:

- **On the Switch,** a small background service (a *sysmodule*, which runs alongside games on homebrewed consoles) announces itself on your Wi-Fi network and handles save reads and writes.
- **On the Mac,** a C++ daemon finds the Switch, pairs with it once, and decides which way each save should go. A menu-bar app shows what happened.

Sync is the dangerous part, since a bug can eat a save file, so that core stays small and boring. Every write is backed up first, and if both sides changed since the last sync, WarpPoint asks instead of guessing.

## What rides on top

Once the link exists, other features are cheap to add, because they reuse the same connection:

- **Chronicle:** every save you've ever had, labeled with where you were in the game.
- **Lens:** a game crashes on the Switch, and the explanation is already waiting on your Mac.
- **Presence:** your Discord shows what you're playing on the Switch.

Before anything on this site, there was Pokémon Jasper: a fangame built in RPG Maker with Pokémon Essentials, a community toolkit written in Ruby. I was about twelve.

The part I spent the most time on was the sprites. Jasper had fusion Pokémon, and each one was two existing sprites cut together and recolored by hand, pixel by pixel, until they looked like one creature.

I posted it on GameJolt, and eventually a DMCA notice took it down. That was my first lesson in shipping: putting a thing out there is the whole point, and it's also how it gets noticed.
